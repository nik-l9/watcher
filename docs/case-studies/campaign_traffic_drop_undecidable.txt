HYPOTHESES TESTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [contradicted] A product or website code change (deploy/regression) around mid-June broke or degraded the signup flow, causing the drop.
      No commits were found in acme/product (June 10-30) or acme/company-website (June 10-20) that could account for a signup-flow regression, and the conversion rate for visitors who did land (Paid vs Organic, both ~4.1%) was unchanged, arguing against a broken funnel.
  [inconclusive] A paid marketing campaign ('spring-2026-brand') was cut back or ended around June 15, reducing paid traffic volume and therefore signups.
      The campaign's session volume fell ~75% on June 15, the same day Paid Search overall fell 74% in the period split and the same day the signup level shift began. Conversion rates were unchanged, so this reads as a volume effect from less paid traffic rather than a worse-converting funnel.
  [contradicted] A general market or seasonal decline in organic demand drove the signup drop.
      Organic Search sessions and conversions were flat (-0.25%/-0.26%) across the same split, so demand outside the paid channel did not move.
