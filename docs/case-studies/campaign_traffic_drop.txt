HYPOTHESES TESTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [inconclusive] The signup drop was caused by pausing paid ads on June 14 after the spring campaign budget was exhausted, which collapsed Paid Search session volume.
      The pause announcement (June 14) precedes the signup level shift (June 15) by one day. Paid Search sessions and conversions collapsed 74% in the same window while Organic Search was flat, and per-session conversion rate was unchanged for both channels -- consistent with a pure traffic-volume shock from a channel that was intentionally shut off, not a funnel or product defect.
  [contradicted] A product or mobile app defect (deploy, bug, broken signup flow) caused the drop.
      No commits exist in acme/product or acme/mobile anywhere in June, ruling out a deploy-driven regression as the cause of the mid-June shift.
  [contradicted] A marketing site change (e.g. broken signup page or CTA) on the website caused the drop.
      No commits to acme/company-website occurred between June 10 and June 20, the window bracketing the level shift.
  [inconclusive] Organic demand itself declined in mid-June (e.g. seasonal effect), independent of paid channel changes.
      Organic Search sessions and conversions were flat (-0.25%, -0.26%) across the two halves of June, showing no broad demand softening outside the paid channel.
